Personal project · private code

A personal finance pipeline that runs on a schedule, gathers my financial documents and emails me a monthly report.

  • Downloads payslips from a payroll portal with Playwright, and card statements from Gmail (OAuth, read-only).
  • Parses every PDF and reconciles it against its own totals, so a misread number never makes it into a report.
  • Estimates installments of a loan indexed to inflation (Unidades Indexadas) from public data — within $1 on months with real statements.
  • Sends a monthly HTML report: income, card spending, installments still owed and a 12-month trend.
